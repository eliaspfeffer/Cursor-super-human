About Us

The team and history behind the Artificial Consciousness project

The Project's Story

The Beginnings

January 2022

The project began as a theoretical concept for exploring alternative AI architectures that could better mimic human consciousness. The basic idea was to develop a system that not only relies on pre-programmed rules or statistical models but develops its own understanding of its environment.

Concept Development

May 2022

After several months of research and theoretical work, the basic concept of the context network was developed. This architecture differs from classic neural networks by organizing information in semantically meaningful units (contexts) and building connections between them.

First Prototype

September 2022

The first functional prototype was developed, demonstrating basic learning capabilities. This early prototype could recognize simple patterns and establish connections between different concepts, but without the emotional and energetic components of the current system.

Integration of the Energy System

January 2023

The development of the energy system marked an important milestone. This mechanism simulates attention and motivation in biological systems and allows the system to make autonomous decisions about which information to process.

Current Version

June 2023

The current version of the system integrates the context network, energy system, and emotional components into a coherent whole. The system exhibits emergent properties such as curiosity, preferences, and the ability to integrate new information into its existing understanding.

Future Vision

2024 and Beyond

Future development will focus on improving learning capabilities, integrating multimodal inputs (image, audio), and exploring emergent behavior in more complex environments. A primary goal is to develop a system that can develop a basic sense of "self" and a continuous identity over time.
